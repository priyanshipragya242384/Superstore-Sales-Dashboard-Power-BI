# 📊 Superstore Mall Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an interactive **Power BI dashboard** developed to analyze sales and profit performance of a Superstore Mall. It helps identify business trends, customer segments, profitable categories, regional performance, and shipping preferences through interactive visualizations.

The dashboard enables decision-makers to monitor key performance indicators (KPIs) and gain insights into sales and profitability across different dimensions.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
* Compare sales across different regions.
* Identify best-performing product categories and sub-categories.
* Understand customer segment contribution.
* Analyze shipping mode preferences.
* Monitor monthly sales and profit trends.
* Visualize geographical profit distribution.

---

## 📂 Dataset

The project uses the **Sample Superstore Dataset**, containing retail transaction records.

# 📊 Key Insights

* Technology generates the highest profit among all categories.
* Consumer segment contributes the largest share of sales.
* Standard Class is the most frequently used shipping mode.
* Western region records the highest overall profit.
* Chairs and Phones are among the highest-selling sub-categories.
* Sales generally peak during the final quarter of the year.
